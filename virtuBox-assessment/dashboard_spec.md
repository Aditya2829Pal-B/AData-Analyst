# Looker Studio Dashboard Specification

## KPI cards
| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Gross revenue | £19.64M |
| Analysis-ready sales rows | 1,003,340 |
| Identified customers | 5,852 |
| Return/cancellation value rate | 3.65% |

## Visuals
1. Monthly gross revenue — line chart; annotate November peaks and partial Dec-2011.
2. RFM revenue share — bar chart; highlight Champions at 69%.
3. Cohort repeat rate — line chart; show month 1–12 behaviour.
4. Lifecycle flow — grouped bars for new, retained, resurrected and lapsed customers.
5. Returns/cancellations — monthly column chart.
6. Country revenue — top-country bar chart.

## Filters
Year, Month, Country, RFM Segment, Product/StockCode.

## Management message
The dashboard is designed to answer: where is revenue concentrated, which customers should be protected or reactivated, when is demand highest, and where do cancellations create operational risk?

## Google requirement
The workbook's Q8 sheet contains the dashboard source/specification. Connect the processed Google Sheet to Looker Studio and publish a shareable report from the candidate's Google account.
